## Reliability Considerations

- PEACE prohibits leading or coercive questioning.
- Do not interrupt the initial free account.
- Present inconsistencies factually, not as accusations.
- Confidence ≠ accuracy; corroborate critical details independently.

## Post-Interview Actions

- Transcribe and store the recording per chain-of-custody protocol.
- Cross-check key claims against independent evidence.
- Complete the evaluation step in the case file; re-interview only if new information emerges.
