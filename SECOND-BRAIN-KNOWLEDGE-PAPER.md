# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Suspect Tracing & Fugitive Investigation Support

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. D. K. Rossmo (2000). Geographic Profiling. CRC Press — Foundational geographic-profiling methodology.
2. L. E. Cohen, M. Felson (1979). Social Change and Crime Rate Trends: A Routine Activity Approach. American Sociological Review — Routine activity theory.
3. P. Brantingham, P. Brantingham (1981). Environmental Criminology. Sage — Crime pattern theory foundations.
4. K. Bennell, S. Corey (2007). Geographic Profiling of Terrorist Attacks. In Criminal Profiling: International Theory, Research, and Practice — Applied geographic profiling.
5. M. K. Sparrow (1991). The Application of Network Analysis to Criminal Intelligence: An Assessment of the Prospects. Social Networks — Foundational link-analysis methodology for investigations.
6. R. E. Fisher, R. P. Geiselman (1992). Memory-Enhancing Techniques for Investigative Interviewing. Charles C. Thomas — Cognitive interview methodology.
7. D. Canter, D. Youngs (2009). Investigative Psychology: Offender Profiling and the Analysis of Criminal Action. Wiley — Empirical investigative-psychology framework.
8. J. Ratcliffe (2004). Geocoding Crime and a First Estimate of a Minimum Acceptable Hit Rate. International Journal of Geographical Information Science — Methodological standard for geographic crime analysis.
9. National Institute of Justice (2000). Crime Mapping: Principle and Practice. U.S. Dept of Justice — Standard crime-mapping/investigative methodology guide.
10. C. Bond (2012). Investigative Interviewing. In Handbook of Forensic Psychology — Structured interview methodology review.
11. K. Rossmo, S. Rombouts (2016). Geographic Profiling. In Environmental Criminology and Crime Analysis (2nd ed.) — Updated methodology review.
12. National Research Council (2009). Strengthening Forensic Science in the United States. National Academies Press — Standards/limitations of forensic investigative methods.
13. M. Bouchard, R. Nash (2015). Researching Terrorism and Counter-Terrorism through a Network Lens. In Social Networks, Terrorism and Counter-terrorism — Network-analysis methodology for investigations.
14. H. Copes, A. Hochstetler (2003). Situational Construction of Masculinity Among Male Street Thieves. Journal of Contemporary Ethnography — Behavioral-pattern research relevant to lead analysis (used cautiously).
15. R. V. Clarke, D. B. Cornish (1985). Modeling Offenders' Decisions: A Framework for Research and Policy. Crime and Justice — Rational-choice framework for behavior-pattern analysis.
16. G. Gudjonsson (2003). The Psychology of Interrogations and Confessions. Wiley — Ethics and reliability considerations in investigative interviewing.
17. J. E. Eck (2005). Crime Hot Spots: What They Are, Why We Have Them, and How to Map Them. In Mapping Crime: Understanding Hot Spots — Hotspot analysis methodology.
18. D. Weisburd, L. Green (1995). Policing Drug Hot Spots: The Jersey City Drug Market Analysis Experiment. Justice Quarterly — Empirical hotspot-based investigative strategy.
19. Association of Chief Police Officers (UK) (2012). Practice Advice on Analytical Techniques for Financial Investigators. NPIA — Applied lead-prioritization methodology.
20. R. Innes (2003). Investigating Murder: Detective Work and the Police Response to Criminal Homicide. Oxford University Press — Empirical study of real-world investigative decision processes.
